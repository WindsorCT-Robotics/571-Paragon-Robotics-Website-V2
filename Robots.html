REEFSCAPE

This year marked an interesting turning point for our team. We​ decided our drivetrain needed a change, and went from Tank Drive to Swerve Drive for our robot Mazu. Based on the Chinese Goddess. For our first year using swerve, we were ranked third and a Finalist for our first competition of the year at Western New England. Our season ended with us being rank 15 in New England FIRST District Championship - Ballard Division.
