MISSON STATEMENT

Paragon Robotics aims to equip students with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to solve problems through competitive design challenges.  We use mentor-led hands-on engineering to build not just robots, but creativity, teamwork, and leadership.

ABOUT OUR TEAM

Paragon Robotics ​is 501.c.3 nonprofit FIRST® Robotics Competition  community team located in Windsor, CT comprised of high school students from Windsor, Bloomfield, East Granby, and more. The team was founded in 2001 and prides itself on giving every student the opportunity to pursue their interests with hands-on, real world engineering design and manufacturing experiences. We are relatively a small team, but that affords us the flexibility to give every student unique attention.
Students are also responsible for raising the funds necessary to support the team and this primarily comes from the generous contributions from our local businesses and organizations. This responsibility gives students the opportunity to grow in communication, team work, and self confidence and gives them a sense of ownership in the team.
